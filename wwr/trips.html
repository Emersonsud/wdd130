Our Main River Adventures

Family Splash Trip

Perfect for families and beginners. Enjoy a scenic, relaxing float with calm Class I-II rapids and beautiful canyon views.

Half Day Adventure Trip

This fast-paced trip packs roaring Class II-III rapids and stunning canyon views into a action-filled morning or afternoon.

Full Day Thrill Trip

This full day journey takes you through exclusive, remote river canyons with big waves and technical rapids ranging from Class II to IV.Recharge with a delicious riverside lunch and quick swim before heading back.

The Grand Rapids Adventure

A multi-day thrilling trip featuring technical Class III-IV rapids, isolated river canyons, hidden waterfalls, and the canyon's most continuous rapids. Includes riverside camping under the stars, meals, and historic trail hiking. Designed for intermediate rafters and veterans. Prior Class III+ experience highly recommended.

Great Water Rampage

This multi-day high-intensity run is designed for adrenaline veterans that have prior Class IV+ experience (mandatory requirement). This expedition tackles rock-choked gorges, violent drops, and long Class V rapids.Experience camping on remote riverbanks, hiking historic trails, exploring hidden waterfalls, and sharing gear maintenance duties and meals.
